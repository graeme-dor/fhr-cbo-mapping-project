About road distances

Driving distances use OpenStreetMap roads through OSRM. Off-road access and live traffic are excluded. Nearest means shortest reported driving-route distance among CBOs in the same province. This is a distance scenario and does not consider capacity. FHR schools are shown as assigned markers only.
